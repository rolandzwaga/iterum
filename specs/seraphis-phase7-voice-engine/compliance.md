### 4.2 Testability constraints the shipped components forced

- **T004/T008/T012/T014 — freeze:** `AtmosphereEngine` bypasses the freeze oscillator whenever the mix
  ramp is settled dry, and `unfreeze()` only *arms* a one-hop fade, so any test of capture/release must
  lift `setFreezeMix` off its FR-019 default of 0 first. `captureFreeze()` is also a no-op until the ring
  holds a whole window, and `advanceOneChunkLifeOnly` never writes the ring — so "every voice captured" is
  unsatisfiable on an idle pool and the FR-030a case runs a note on every one of the 16 slots.
- **T004 — morph handoff:** `cloud().getPartialTargetAmplitude(i)` is not equal to
